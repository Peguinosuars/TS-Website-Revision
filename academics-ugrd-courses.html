Undergraduate Program

The Undergraduate Program in Technological Systems Management provides students with a unique interdisciplinary foundation. The program focuses on understanding, assessing, and managing technology effectively within societal frameworks, preparing students for innovative leadership across diverse technology sectors.

Through rigorous hands-on coursework, foundational engineering design concepts, and advanced research projects, students explore complex modern challenges such as sustainable technology, system planning, and technical communication.

The undergraduate academic catalog outlines standard academic policies, department degree requirements, credit allocation procedures, and the entire repository of undergraduate learning paths available at the university.
