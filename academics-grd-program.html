Graduate Program

Accelerated BS/MS Program Overview

The Accelerated BS/MS program allows highly qualified SUNY Korea undergraduate students to fast-track their graduate studies, completing both degrees in an expedited timeline.

Master of Science (MS) Program

The MS degree in Technological Systems Management prepares students to lead innovation, manage complex technical systems, and understand the socio-economic impacts of technology.

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) Program

The PhD program focuses on advanced independent research in Technology, Policy, and Innovation (TPI), developing global scholars and policy experts.
