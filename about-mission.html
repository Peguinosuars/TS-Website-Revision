Mission & History & Vision

About / Mission & History & Vision

🎯 Our Vision & Global Identity

The Technology and Society (TS) at SUNY Korea is an extension of the same department at Stony Brook University in New York, sharing the same mission, curriculum, and degrees. Our ultimate mission is to train you to make creative and innovative solutions for future society through global, interdisciplinary, and trans-disciplinary knowledge.

📌 Our Core Mission Aims

01. 21st Century Education

Providing a digital and holistic ‘big picture’ education coupled with specific technical learning (ICT, AMS, CS) for practical knowledge.

02. Global Economy Readiness

Equipping students with knowledge that dynamically keeps up with a constantly changing global economy.

03. Career Practicality

Focusing on actual industry readiness through tailored opportunities such as high-value internships and job placements.

04. Critical Leadership Roles

Nurturing critical working and thinking abilities aligned with "4.0" economy needs (targeting paths toward CTO, CDO, CIO positions).

05. Creative Decision Making

Developing strategic skills to become innovative decision-makers in business, policy, or advanced technical fields.

06. Social Consciousness & Ethics

Developing globalized citizen engagement to solve socio-technical problems for social good, while staying highly aware of ethical issues.

🌐 Why Our Global Environment Strategy Matters

• Cultural Diversity: An international basis of students with over 30 nationalities attending our campus (as of 2020), offering an environment vastly unique from other universities in Korea.

• SBU Prestige & Uniqueness: Benefit from a unique professional network and a degree from Stony Brook University. TS at SUNY Korea is the only such department in Korea and one of the few worldwide.

• Songdo Green Smart City: Direct links to unique communities and work opportunities in South Korea's premier tech hub, Songdo, paired with global scholarship support.

⏳ History of TS at SUNY Korea

The Department of Technology and Society is the oldest and largest of departments introduced to SUNY Korea by Stony Brook University.

2019
09
Fresh Undergraduate Specialization in ICT4SD

The new ICT4SD undergraduate specialization is implemented. Students can choose from three paths: AMS, CSE, and ICT4SD.

08
First Interchange of Faculty Between SBU & SUNY Korea

Gerry Stokes becomes the Chair of TS at SUNY Korea, launching long-term faculty interchange from Stony Brook University.

2018
01
Fresh Graduate Courses in Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR)

Under Chair James Larson, a new focus in DRR was added to the M.S. degree, creating two solid tracks: Global Technology Management and DRR.

2017
12.01
First Graduation of Undergraduate Students

Graduation of the first undergraduate class with a B.S. in Technological Systems Management, addressed by SBU President Stanley.

2016
03.01
Undergraduate Curriculum Innovation

Under Chair James Larson, three new big data and mobile infrastructure courses were added to launch the ICT4SD undergrad focus.

2013
06.29
First Graduation of Master's Students

The historic first graduation ceremony for students completing their M.S. degrees occurred.

03.04
First Day of Undergraduate Classes

First Day of Classes for the Charter Cohort (B.S. degree). 26 elite students enrolled from South Korea, the US, and Chile.

2012

MEST Authorization

Operation of the undergraduate program officially approved by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MEST).

03.02

First Day of Classes for the graduate program (M.S.). Addressed by Former Chair Prof. Fred Phillips and First President Kim Choonho.
