Academic Regulations

⚠️ Leave of Absence & Graduation Requirements (Catalog Year)

Taking a Leave of Absence (LOA) for two or more semesters will automatically change your Required Term, altering your graduation requirements to the latest version that you rematriculated.

  • 🎖️ Military Leave Exception: To maintain your original graduation requirements, you must officially select "Military LOA" when applying for leave, and submit an official Military Certificate upon rematriculation for validation.
    🚨 Crucial Warning (The One-Semester Rule):
    Every single semester of your absence must be fully covered by the Military Certificate. If there is even a single semester left uncovered by your official service dates, your Required Term will change automatically without exception.
